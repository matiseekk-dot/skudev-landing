// Vinyl collection and concert tracker · Android

Your records. Your profit. Your shows.

A vinyl collection with real Discogs market value for every pressing, a bridge between Spotify and Last.fm and your shelf, a concert calendar with festival Live Mode, and push notifications when a favorite band gets a repress or plays your city.

Get it on Google Play
on Google Play · EN/PL/DE · no ads
// why this app

My vinyl is scattered across three different places, and I never knew what the whole collection was actually worth. Discogs shows you the price of one record, not the sum of all of them, split by pressing and edition on top of that. I started logging what I had, then added concerts too, so I would never miss a favorite band in town.

Mateusz, vinyl collector, maker
// what you get

Everything other trackers were missing.

01
💿

Discogs value, per pressing

Real market value for every record, tracked separately for vinyl color, pressing and edition. Your collection total moves with the market, not just with what you paid.

02
🎧

Spotify/Last.fm to vinyl bridge

The app spots the artists you stream the most who are not on your shelf yet. Your buy list builds itself.

03
🎫

Concerts with a festival mode

Add your favorite bands' shows and get reminders ahead of time. At a festival, Live Mode shows who is playing now and what's next.

04
🎲

Vinyl Roulette

Not sure what to spin tonight? One tap picks a record from your collection, weighted toward the ones that have been sitting on the shelf the longest.

05
🔔

Push notifications

A repress of a favorite album, a price drop on Discogs, an upcoming show. Notifications land on your phone even when the app is closed.

06
💰

Realized profit from sales

A dedicated stat shows how much you actually made from records you sold, not just the estimated value of what's left on the shelf.

07
📦

Pre order and delivery status

Tap the badge to mark a record as ordered. The app tracks the release date and reminds you to look out for the package.

08
🎁

Gifted records

Flag a record as a gift and it stops counting toward your spending or your for sale collection.

09
🌍

3 languages, no ads

English, Polish and German. The core app stays free forever, Pro unlocks extended stats and personalization.

// inside the app

Collection, valuation and Vault Score in one screen.

// get it

Free, no ads, in 3 languages.

Install it from Google Play and start adding records right away: by hand, by scanning the barcode on the sleeve, or by syncing with Discogs.
