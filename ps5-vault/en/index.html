// Game collection tracker · Android

Your games. Your money. Your time.

Your collection with cover art from RAWG, the real cost per game and in total, hours played and a release calendar. Import your whole library from Steam, Xbox, PSN or Playnite. No account and no ads, your collection stays on your phone.

Get it on Google Play
on Google Play · free · Polish, English, Spanish
// why this app

I've had a PS5 since launch. I buy games on sale, open some of them, most never. I bought the same game three times before I realized that was a problem. I built the app to know what I own, and it quickly turned out the more interesting part is what it all cost.

Mateusz, PS5 owner and the app's creator
// what you get

Everything I was missing in other trackers.

01
💰

Finances with the real cost

A separate tab. The real cost of your collection, cost per hour, ROI on games you sold, spending charts by store and genre. You see which game was your best investment per hour played.

02
🔁

Import your whole library

Paste or upload an export from Steam, Xbox, PSN or Playnite. The app matches games against the RAWG database and sets statuses from hours played and trophy progress. Hundreds of games in a few minutes.

03
📅

Releases with a countdown

Games with a release date sort themselves. Reminders a month and a week before release, in the last three days and on launch day, when you open the app. Pre-orders don't count toward the month's spending before release.

04
🎮

Statuses, platinums, subscriptions

Playing · Completed · Planning · Abandoned, plus a Platinum toggle 🏆 for completed games. Games from PS Plus, Game Pass or EA Play don't count toward spending. Multi-platform: PS5, PS4, Xbox, PC, Switch, Mobile.

05
💳

Monthly budget

Set how much you want to spend on games each month. The app adds up what you spent on games, DLC and microtransactions this month and warns you when you go over.

06
🔍

Search and sort

Search by title, filter by status, platform and source, sort by date added, rating, hours or completion date. Stays smooth even with 700+ games.

07
🎲

Backlog randomizer

Can't decide what to play? One tap picks a random game from the ones waiting in your queue. Mark it as Playing right away or roll again.

08
🎁

Year in Review

A Spotify-Wrapped-style recap of your year: hours, finished games, platinums and your most-played titles. Share it as an image.

09
🔒

No account, no ads

Your collection lives on your phone. Export a JSON backup whenever you like and import it on another device (merge or replace). The only statistic is an anonymous Cloudflare visit counter, no cookies.
