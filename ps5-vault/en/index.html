// questions

You ask, we answer.

How do I check how much I spent on PS Store?

On the console: Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Payment and Subscriptions → Transaction History. You can see the same history by signing in to your PlayStation account in a browser. Enter the amounts with your games in PS5 Vault and the app adds them up with DLC and microtransactions.

What is a pile of shame?

Games you bought (often on sale) and never started. PS5 Vault counts how many you have, what you paid for them and which one has waited the longest. You can share the result as an image.

Does the app connect to my PSN account?

No. You need no account and no sign-in. You import your PSN library by copying the table from the public PSN-Profiles site. Everything stays on your phone.

Is it only for PS5?

No. Add games from PS5, PS4, Xbox, PC, Switch and mobile, and import from Steam, PSN, Xbox and Playnite.

Is PS5 Vault free?

Yes. Collection, finances, pile of shame, releases, wishlist and year in games are free with no ads. Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) with unlimited import, cost per hour, a budget and the serial scanner.

Which phones does it run on?

Android, from Google Play.
