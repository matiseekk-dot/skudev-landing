// why this app

I've had a PS5 since launch. I buy games on sale, open some of them, most never. I bought the same game three times before I realized that was a problem. I built the app to know what I own, and it quickly turned out the more interesting part is what it all cost.

Mateusz, PS5 owner and the app's creator

Everything I was missing in other trackers.

Finances with the real cost

A separate tab. Free: the real cost of your collection and this month's spending. Pro: cost per hour, ROI on games you sold, a year forecast and spending charts by store and genre. You see which game was your best investment per hour played.

Games with a release date sort themselves. Reminders a month and a week before release, in the last three days and on launch day, when you open the app. Pre-orders don't count toward the month's spending before release.

Playing · Completed · Planning · Abandoned, plus a Platinum toggle 🏆 for completed games. Games from PS Plus, Game Pass or EA Play don't count toward spending. Multi-platform: PS5, PS4, Xbox, PC, Switch, Mobile.

Set how much you want to spend on games each month. The app adds up what you spent on games, DLC and microtransactions this month and warns you when you go over. A Pro feature.

Search by title, filter by status, platform and source, sort by date added, rating, hours or completion date. Stays smooth even with 700+ games.

// questions

Frequently asked questions.

How do I check how much I spent on the PlayStation Store?

On PS5: Settings → Users and Accounts → Account → Payment and Subscriptions → Transaction History. Pick the account and a date range, and the console lists your purchases, newest first. In a browser, sign in to PlayStation Account Management and open Transaction History. Copy the amounts into PS5 Vault and the app works out the real cost of your collection and the cost per hour of gaming.

How do I work out what an hour of gaming costs me?

Divide the game price (minus what you get back by selling it) by the hours you played. A $70 game played for 50 hours costs $1.40 per hour. Use the calculator above, and PS5 Vault does it automatically for every game in your collection.

Does PS5 Vault connect to my PSN account?

No. The app never asks for your PSN login or password. You import your PlayStation library by copying the games table from psnprofiles.com, and everything stays on your phone.

Can I track Steam, Xbox and PC games too?

Yes. Import works with Steam, Xbox (via TrueAchievements), PSN (via PSN-Profiles) and Playnite, which gathers libraries from many stores. Platforms in the app: PS5, PS4, Xbox, PC, Switch and mobile.

What does PS5 Vault Pro include and how much is it?

Pro is a one-time purchase on Google Play, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited import, advanced finances, the monthly budget and the batch barcode scanner. The app shows the price in your currency before you buy. Everything else is free.

Does PS5 Vault work on iPhone?

The app is on Google Play for Android. On iPhone and computers you can use the browser version: matiseekk-dot.github.io/Games. Buying Pro works only in the Google Play app.

How do I move my collection to a new phone?

In Settings choose Export data, then on the new phone choose Import data and pick that file. You can merge the collections or replace one with the other.
