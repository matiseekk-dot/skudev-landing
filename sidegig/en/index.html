// Side income · web and Android

What your side hustle really earns.

Freelance gigs, selling on Vinted and eBay, records, games and books, investments, rentals — everything you do outside your salary, in one profit and loss statement. With costs and fees, in any currency, month by month.

Open Sidegig What it does
free · no ads · no sign-up needed · 9 languages
// why this app

I started with a plain expense tracker. It quickly turned out the better question was different: what do the things I do after hours actually bring in? A bank shows one list of payments. Sidegig counts every source separately, with fees and costs, so you can see what's left.

Mateusz, maker

// what you get

Less typing, more answers.

01
📈

Net side income

One number up front: what your side hustle earned this month after costs. Next to it, last month, the year so far and your goal. Use the arrows to go back to any month.

02
🏷️

Sales with fees

What you paid, what you sold it for, what the platform and shipping took. You see the real profit on every item, not just the amount that hit your account.

03
💿

Collections with valuations

Import your records from Discogs together with market prices. Every item shows its paper gain, and every collection shows what you spent on it in a given month and year.

04
📷

Barcode scanner

The camera reads the ISBN or EAN from the cover and the title and author fill in by themselves. Add a whole shelf in one go and enter what you paid — the expense is recorded for you.

05
📊

Investments the way your broker shows them

Enter the average price and the number of units, or simply the value and profit from your broker's app. Crypto and gold prices update by themselves; ETFs and stocks do in the Android app.

06
🏠

Rentals without a spreadsheet

Rent for every month, arrears and the costs of a flat, garage or parking space. You can see at a glance what has been paid and what hasn't.

07
🔔

Reminders

An invoice due date, a subscription renewal, rent from a tenant. On Android the reminder arrives even when the app is closed.

08
💱

Any currency

A gig paid in euros, a record from the UK, a trip in crowns. Every entry keeps its own currency, and totals use the exchange rate from the day of the transaction.

09
🔒

Your data, your rules

Try it without signing in. A Google account is only used to sync between devices. No ads, no analytics, Excel export, and account deletion with one button.
