How many wet diapers a day is a good sign?

Updated: September 27, 2026 · Based on: American Academy of Pediatrics
In short

From about day 5 of life, a baby usually has at least 6 wet diapers (nappies) a day. Fewer wet diapers, a dry diaper for more than 6 hours, crying without tears or a sunken soft spot may mean dehydration and are a reason to contact a doctor.

The first days after birth

In the first days there are fewer wet diapers because there is still little milk. The number grows day by day, and from about day 5 there should be at least 6 a day.

Signs of dehydration

  • A dry diaper for more than 6 hours
  • Crying without tears
  • A sunken soft spot (fontanelle) on the head
  • Fewer than 6 wet diapers a day
When to see a doctor

With signs of dehydration, contact a doctor, especially with a young baby. Persistent vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than a day in a baby is also a reason to call, because it quickly leads to dehydration.
