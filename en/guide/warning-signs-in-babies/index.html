Warning signs in babies: when not to wait until morning

Updated: September 27, 2026 · Based on: American Academy of Pediatrics
In short

Don’t wait until morning if a baby under 3 months has a fever of 38°C (100.4°F) or more, or if a child is lethargic and hard to wake, has trouble breathing or bluish lips, has a seizure, a stiff neck or a rash that does not fade when pressed. Get urgent help: go to the emergency room or call your local emergency number.

Urgent: ER or emergency number

  1. Fever of 38°C (100.4°F) or more in a baby under 3 months, even with no other symptoms.
  2. Temperature of 40.5°C (105°F) or more at any age.
  3. Lethargy, difficulty waking, not responding to voice or touch.
  4. Trouble breathing, wheezing, bluish lips.
  5. Seizures, a stiff neck or a rash that does not fade when pressed.

How to check if a rash fades

Press the side of a clear glass firmly against the rash. If the spots are still clearly visible through the glass, don’t wait: this needs urgent help.

Call your doctor

  • Signs of dehydration: a dry diaper for more than 6 hours, crying without tears, a sunken soft spot on the head.
  • Persistent vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than a day in a baby.
  • Fever lasting more than 72 hours in a child over 6 months.
Emergency numbers

Use your local emergency number: 112 across the European Union, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada. In the UK, NHS 111 helps with urgent problems that are not life threatening.
