    How the due date is calculated

    Doctors count pregnancy from the first day of your last period, not from conception. The due date is that day plus 280 days, or 40 weeks (Naegele’s rule). If your cycle is longer or shorter than 28 days, the due date moves by the difference.

    An ultrasound due date is more accurate

    An ultrasound in the first trimester gives the most accurate due date. If your doctor set your due date from a scan, choose “ultrasound due date” and the calculator will work out your pregnancy week from it.

    Few babies arrive exactly on the due date

    The due date is an estimate. A pregnancy is full term from 37 weeks, and a birth between 37 and 42 completed weeks counts as on time. That’s why it’s worth having your hospital bag ready by about 36 weeks.

    Frequently asked questions

    When does pregnancy week counting start?

    From the first day of your last period. That’s why at conception you are already about 3 weeks pregnant.

    What does 24w 3d mean?

    24 full weeks and 3 days. You are then in week 25 of pregnancy.

    How long is each trimester?

    The first trimester lasts until the end of week 13 (13w 6d), the second until the end of week 27 (27w 6d), and the third from week 28 until birth.
