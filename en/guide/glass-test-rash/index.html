The glass test: rash and fever in a child

Updated: September 29, 2026 · Based on: NHS, Meningitis Research Foundation
In short

Press a clear glass against the rash and look through it. If the spots fade, keep watching your child and call your pediatrician if there is a fever. If the spots do NOT fade under pressure, call emergency services right away: it could be meningococcal disease.

How to do the glass test

  1. Take a clear glass with smooth sides.
  2. Press the side of the glass firmly against the rash.
  3. Look through the glass to see whether the spots fade and disappear under pressure.
ResultWhat you seeWhat to do
Spots fadethey disappear under the glassWatch your child. With a fever, call your pediatrician.
Spots do NOT fadestill visible through the glassCall emergency services now

Darker skin

A rash can be harder to see on darker skin. Check paler areas: the palms, the soles of the feet, inside the eyelids and the roof of the mouth.

The test is not everything

Early on, the rash may still fade. If your child seems very unwell, is drowsy, has cold hands and feet or is getting worse quickly, call emergency services (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU) whatever the glass test shows. Full list: warning signs in babies.
