Hospital bag checklist for labour

Updated: October 3, 2026 · Based on: NHS
In short

Pack your hospital bag by about 36 weeks. The essentials are your maternity notes, things for you for 2 to 3 days and an outfit for your baby to go home in. Every hospital has its own list, so check your maternity unit’s website.

📅Calculate your due date›

Documents

  • Your maternity notes (paper notes or the app your maternity service uses)
  • Test results and scan reports, including your blood group
  • Your birth plan, if you have one
  • Photo ID and insurance details, if your hospital asks for them

For you

  • 2 or 3 front-opening nightdresses or big T-shirts that are easy to breastfeed in
  • A dressing gown, slippers and flip-flops for the shower
  • Comfortable maternity pads and disposable or loose cotton underwear
  • A nursing bra and breast pads
  • Toiletries, a towel and a hair tie
  • Water and light snacks
  • A phone charger with a long cable
  • Loose clothes to go home in: your bump doesn’t disappear straight away

For your baby

  • 2 or 3 sets of bodysuits and sleepsuits in newborn size
  • A hat and socks
  • Newborn nappies and wipes, if the hospital doesn’t provide them
  • A blanket
  • An outfit to go home in, suitable for the weather
  • A rear-facing car seat: leave it in the car until you go home

For your birth partner

  • A change of comfortable clothes and shoes
  • Water and snacks
  • A card or change for parking and vending machines
  • A charger
When should I pack?

By about 36 weeks. A pregnancy is full term from 37 weeks, and labour can start earlier than you expect. When to go in: when to go to the hospital in labour.
