How much should a baby sleep?

Updated: September 27, 2026 · Based on: National Sleep Foundation, American Academy of Pediatrics
In short

Up to 3 months, a baby sleeps 14 to 17 hours a day in total, from 4 to 11 months 12 to 15 hours, and at 1 to 2 years 11 to 14 hours, naps included.

Sleep per day by age

Child’s ageSleep per day
0 to 3 months14 to 17 hours
4 to 11 months12 to 15 hours
1 to 2 years11 to 14 hours
3 to 5 years10 to 13 hours

These ranges cover the whole day, naps included. Every child sleeps differently, so treat them as a reference, not a rule.

Newborns wake often

A newborn sleeps in short stretches and wakes every 1 to 3 hours, mostly to feed. The day and night rhythm usually settles during the first 2 months. Bright days and dark, quiet nights help.

Worse sleep around 4 months

Around 4 months, sleep often gets worse for a while. It is a natural stage of brain development, not a parenting mistake.
