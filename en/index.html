// why this app

Our child got a fever in the middle of the night. I searched Google for what to do, and every site said something different. I wanted everything in one place: readings, medicines given with the time and thresholds from pediatric guidelines. That is how Calm Parent was born.

Mateusz, dad and creator of the app
